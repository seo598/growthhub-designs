Home/What we publish

What we publish, and what we don’t.

Most workspace sites fill their gaps with stock photography and invented availability. This is the register instead — generated from the same checks our own team is held to, not written by hand.

Information this site publishes and information it does not yet hold
InformationStatusWhy
Office sizes, capacities and monthly ratesPublishedSourced and on the site.
Included meeting hours, per officePublishedSourced and on the site.
Add-on pricingPublishedSourced and on the site.
The ecosystem partner listPublishedSourced and on the site.
The street address and mapNot publishedThe business has not given us a postal address to publish. We would rather leave it blank than print one we cannot stand behind.
Map locationNot publishedWithout confirmed coordinates a map would put a pin in roughly the right place, which is worse than no map at all.
Hourly room ratesNot publishedNo hourly rate card has been supplied, so rooms show “on request” and the team quotes you directly.
How many offices are free right nowNot publishedWe know how many offices each collection holds, but not how many are free today.
Live availabilityNot publishedOccupancy is not connected to this site yet, so you will not see “only 2 left” anywhere on it.
Photography of the spacesNot publishedEvery photograph on this site was taken inside a Growth Hub centre. Where we have no photograph of a space, we show a mark rather than a picture of something else.
Our phone number, confirmedNot publishedThe number is on the site, but it has not been reconfirmed with the team, so we keep it out of the data search engines read.
Membership plan pricingNot publishedThe plan tiers exist; their prices have not been set yet, so joining stays closed rather than quoting a guess.
TestimonialsNot publishedNone have been collected yet. Every one we publish will carry a real name, a real company and written consent.

Why we do it this way

A wrong number is worse than noneStructured data is machine-readable. A price or phone number we haven’t confirmed can be surfaced in search and quoted back at the business, so unconfirmed values stay out of it.
No stock photographyThin, generic imagery is the exact failure we set out to beat. Until there are real photographs of these rooms, there are none.
No invented scarcityOccupancy is not connected yet, so you will not see “only 2 left”. When it is connected, the number will be real.
Something here you can answer?If you are from the Growth Hub team and can supply any of the pending items, send it to us and this page updates itself the moment it lands in the system.
