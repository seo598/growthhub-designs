Membership plans

A plan is a paid benefits tier for people without an office — meeting hours, a booking discount and priority, valid at every centre. An office already bundles hours, so a customer can hold one or the other, never both. Plans are company-wide, not per-venue, so this page is unscoped.

4 active plans have no monthly price: Basic, Professional, Business, Enterprise. The public page shows “on request” and the join button stays disabled until a price is set here.
