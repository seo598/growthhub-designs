Pricing rules

A rule modifies the published rate; it never replaces the need for one. A space with no rate still reads “on request” no matter how many rules exist, so a rule can never invent a price. Rules are born switched off, and switching one on is what decides the date from which it applies.

No pricing rules are liveEvery booking is priced at its published rate. That is a valid state, not a missing one — the source documents supply no multipliers, no peak windows, no season boundaries and no holiday dates, so nothing has been invented here.

Guard rails

The floor is enforced at every step of the calculation, not once at the end — so a deep discount cannot dip below it and then be hidden by a later uplift. Additive composition is the only mode that can cross −100%, which is why the floor exists.

Holiday and season dates

Ramadan and Eid move each year on the lunar calendar, and announced dates are routinely corrected. A correction therefore supersedes rather than edits: the old row is kept so a booking already priced can still be explained.
