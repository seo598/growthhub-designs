About Growth Hub

Growth Hub by Fahdan is a premium multi-floor workspace built for startup founders, SMEs and large enterprises.

Beyond the office space, we offer an integrated business ecosystem designed to support our tenants across business setup, strategy, digital transformation, finance and accounting, sales, marketing, and more. We empower businesses to launch, operate and scale with ease, all under one roof.

Our collections

  • The Collective — 2nd floor, for founders and teams establishing their presence
  • The Elevate — 14th floor, a premium collection with panoramic views
  • The Apex — 13th floor, our most exclusive collection for executive teams

Where we are

We currently operate three centres — Adliya and the Diplomatic Area in the Kingdom of Bahrain, and Al-Khobar in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — with expansion planned across the GCC.
