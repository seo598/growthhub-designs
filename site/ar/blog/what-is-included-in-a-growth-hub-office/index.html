المحتوى معروض بالإنجليزية — الترجمة العربية قيد الإعداد

Flexible workspace pricing is usually quoted low and topped up later. Ours is quoted all-in, so here is the complete list of what a monthly office at Growth Hub Al-Khobar covers.

In every package

  • Electricity and water
  • High-speed WiFi
  • Printing facilities
  • Administration support
  • Reception and mail handling
  • Pantry access — water, coffee and tea
  • Office cleaning services

Your meeting-hour allowance

Every office includes meeting-room time each month: 10 hours on Starter, 15 on Growth, Horizon and both Elevate zones, and 20 on The Apex. Hours are drawn when a booking is confirmed and returned automatically if you cancel. The balance is visible in your dashboard and valid at any Growth Hub centre.

Optional add-ons

  • Gym membership — SAR 600 / month
  • Parking — SAR 600 / month
  • Additional desk — SAR 200 / month
  • Universal incubator access — SAR 500 / month

And the part that isn't square metres

Tenants sit inside a business ecosystem: company formation and licensing, accounting and audit, marketing and go-to-market, and incubation programmes — all delivered by independent companies operating in the same building.

Because your business needs more than four walls.
