Private Offices

6 live spaces across 1 city.

A private office is a lockable room your team occupies on its own, inside a building where reception, cleaning, internet and meeting rooms are already running. You take the room and the building takes care of the rest — the arrangement most companies move to once a shared desk stops being enough.
