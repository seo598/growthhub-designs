A private office is not a planEvery office already carries meeting hours of its own, which is why the two cannot stack. If a room is what you are after, the collections are priced per floor.See the offices →
Tiers named, benefits not yet decidedThe brief names these five tiers and six kinds of benefit — discounted bookings, free meeting hours, priority booking, exclusive workspaces, faster support, lounge access — but never says which benefit belongs to which tier, or what any of them cost. Rather than invent that ladder, the site says so and joining stays disabled. Hours, discounts and priority are set on the admin Plans page, and every one of them works the moment it's filled in.

How a plan differs from an office

An officeA private room at one centre, with 10–20 meeting hours included. From SAR 4,500 a month in Al-Khobar.
A planNo room of your own — meeting hours, a booking discount and priority across every centre.
Not bothAn office already includes hours, so the two can't stack. The site enforces that rather than double-charging.

Start with an account.

A tier attaches to your account, so that is the first step, and making one costs nothing. What you can then join depends on what the account already holds — an office rules a plan out entirely, because it carries meeting hours of its own.
