What makes it different

Four things the competition doesn’t do.

Meeting hours, not just walls Every office includes 10–20 hours a month. The site tracks the balance, draws it down, and returns it on cancellation.
Two searches, not one Monthly offices and hourly rooms are different purchases. Mixing them puts SAR 4,500/month next to SAR 120/hour.
Real availability A live calendar and confirmation, rather than a “request a viewing” form.
Book a desk, register a company Formation, accounting and marketing sit in the same building — an offer a pure marketplace can’t make.
About the data on these screens Office names, sizes, seat counts, prices, included meeting hours and the add-ons are real, from the Al-Khobar catalogue. Meeting-room rates, live unit availability, photography and the street address are placeholders until the business supplies them — each is marked on the screen itself rather than quietly invented.
