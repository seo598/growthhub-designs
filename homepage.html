Questions

Before you book.

What is actually included in the monthly price?

Electricity and water, high-speed WiFi, printing facilities, administration support, reception and mail handling, pantry access with water, coffee and tea, and office cleaning. Plus your monthly meeting-room allowance — 10, 15 or 20 hours depending on the collection.

Can I add parking, a gym membership or another desk?

Yes. Parking and gym membership are SAR 600 a month each, an additional desk is SAR 200, and universal access to all Growth Hub incubators is SAR 500. Add-ons attach to your membership and appear on the same invoice.

Can I use my company address for commercial registration?

Yes — that is the point of being in the ecosystem. Fahdan handles CR, licensing and visas in both Bahrain and Saudi Arabia, and your Growth Hub office is the registered address.

Do my meeting hours work at other centres?

Your allowance is valid at any Growth Hub centre. Book from your dashboard and the balance draws down wherever the room is.

How do I list my own business centre here?

Apply through the Network Partner programme. Once approved you manage your own listings, availability and pricing, and take part in the referral and commission programme.
