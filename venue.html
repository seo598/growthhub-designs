About this venue

Land. Launch. Lead.

Growth Hub by Fahdan is a premium multi-floor workspace in Al-Khobar built for startup founders, SMEs and large enterprises — 61 private offices across three collections, with meeting rooms, pods, an event hall and an open-air meeting area shared across the building.

Beyond the office space, tenants sit inside an integrated business ecosystem covering business setup, strategy, digital transformation, finance & accounting, sales and marketing — so you can launch, operate and scale under one roof.
